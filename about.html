Who We Are

Crafted where it is conceived.

From the first sketch to the final polish, every step of the journey takes place within the walls of our atelier. Master artisans breathe life into raw materials, transforming them into timeless works of art with a blend of traditional techniques and contemporary innovation.

At Alvee Jewels Private Limited, we turn imagination into timeless elegance. As one of India’s leading jewellery manufacturing houses, we specialise in diamond, gold and silver jewellery that celebrates beauty, precision and craftsmanship.

We cater to retailers, wholesalers and private labels across India and global markets through outright sale and job-work models. Each creation undergoes rigorous quality checks to ensure unmatched purity, finishing and design integrity.
