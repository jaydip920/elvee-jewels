Do you work with private labels?

Yes. We manufacture to partner specifications under private-label arrangements and also support job-work models.

What should I include in an RFQ?

Product category, purity, expected volume, target price band, branding needs and any design references. The form above captures all of these.

Can you make pieces from our designs?

Share the design, sample or a clear written brief. We will review feasibility and confirm the production route with you.

Do you publish a catalogue or price list?

Please request the current line sheet and commercial terms using the catalogue button below. Current ranges and pricing are shared directly after we understand your requirements.

When is an order confirmed?

Only after the scope, pricing, quantities and delivery terms are confirmed in writing. Submitting an RFQ does not create a contract.
