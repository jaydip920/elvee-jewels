## 2. Ready-to-paste project description

**ALVEE Jewels — Where design meets precision.**

I redesigned the original Elveester website into a modern-luxury digital experience
for **ALVEE Jewels**, a jewellery manufacturing house based in Surat, India. The goal
was simple: make a heritage atelier feel contemporary, trustworthy and easy to do
business with — without losing the warmth of handcrafted jewellery.

### The brief

The old site had several problems: an API call that failed on every load, a blocked
web font, broken and mixed-content images, no `<main>` landmark, unlabelled buttons,
low-contrast text and forms that did not work reliably. It also had no clear path for
trade buyers, even though a large part of the business is B2B.

### What I built

- **A modernised logo system** — a faceted ALVEE monogram built as clean vector SVG,
  with a jewel-cut centre, double gold frame and coordinated favicon. The mark and
  wordmark are used consistently in the site header, footer and partner guide PDF.
- **A complete 10-page static site** — Home, About, Careers, Contact, Customer
  Services, FAQ, Appointment, Customize, Terms and a new For Business page.
- **An interactive collections explorer** — Diamond, Gold and Silver ranges can be
  switched with tabs, keyboard arrows and proper ARIA tab/tabpanel semantics.
- **A dedicated B2B experience** — partnership models (outright sale, private label
  and job work), supply formats, a five-step process, B2B FAQs and a structured RFQ
  form covering company, market, category, purity, volume, target price, branding and
  timeline.
- **A downloadable partner guide PDF** — a two-page guide explaining which companies
  should use the B2B service and what to prepare before sending an RFQ.
- **Motion with purpose** — GSAP ScrollTrigger powers staggered reveals, count-up
  stats, page-header parallax and the scroll progress bar. An IntersectionObserver
  fallback and `prefers-reduced-motion` support keep the site usable everywhere.
- **Form fixes without a backend** — validated `mailto:` forms open the visitor's
  email app with a properly structured enquiry, so no silent API failure is left
  behind.
- **SEO and accessibility basics** — semantic landmarks, labelled controls, local
  images, descriptive alt text, canonical/OG metadata, robots.txt and sitemap.xml.

### Visual direction

Warm ivory and deep ink with a restrained gold accent, editorial serif headings and
a clean system-font body. Generous white space, soft 8 / 16 / 24px radii and subtle
curved section dividers give the layout a premium feel without looking templated.
All images are stored locally, so the design renders reliably without third-party
hotlinks.

### Outcome

All 10 pages were checked in Chrome: **zero console errors**, no horizontal overflow,
a `<main>` landmark on every page, and **100 / 100 / 100** in Lighthouse for
Accessibility, Best Practices and SEO on the key pages.

*Brand note: the website uses the ALVEE brand name while the original contact email
(info@elvee.in), phone number, address and elveester.com URLs are preserved as-is.*
