From the first sketch to the final polish.

How We Work

A journey of four careful steps.

  1. 01

    Imagine

    Concept, mood and form are explored until the idea feels unmistakably yours.

  2. 02

    Craft

    Master artisans shape the metal by hand, balancing traditional skill with modern tooling.

  3. 03

    Set

    Stones are selected and set with precision, symmetry and a keen eye for detail.

  4. 04

    Polish & Present

    Rigorous quality checks precede the final polish, finishing and handover.
